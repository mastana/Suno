Honest Answers

You probably want to ask...

Is this therapy?
No. Suno is a listening service. Our listeners hear you without judgment — they don't diagnose or treat. If you need clinical help, we connect you to verified professionals.
Is it a real person or AI?
Always a real human. We will never replace your listener with a bot. That's our promise.
Is it confidential?
Completely. First names only. No recordings. What you say stays between you and your listener.
Who are the listeners?
Trained, empathetic individuals — many with backgrounds in psychology, social work, and public health — assessed for their ability to listen with care.

Honest Answers

You probably want to ask...

Is this therapy?
No. Suno is a listening service. Our listeners hear you without judgment, they don't diagnose or treat. When you need clinical help, we connect you to verified professionals.

Always a real human. We will never replace your listener with a bot. That's our promise.
Will anyone know?
No. First names only. No recordings. What you say stays between you and your listener.
What if I don't know what to say?
That's okay. You don't need the right words. Just start anywhere. We'll listen.
